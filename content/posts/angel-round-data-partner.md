We’re excited to announce our partnership with GAIA Real Estate, one of New York’s leading commercial real estate firms. This partnership gives us access to GAIA’s deep expertise in real estate private equity, high-quality real-world data, and more than two decades of hard-earned insight.

Our first joint initiative focuses on CRE underwriting. Together, we’re developing rigorous evaluations using real deal-room materials to assess how frontier AI handles the underwriting process from start to finish.

We’re also pleased to welcome GAIA as the first investor in our angel round. Their backing will help us expand our research and grow our team.
